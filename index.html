Our Mission

Healing isn’t becoming someone new.
It’s remembering who you’ve always been.

Move2Align blends mindful movement, breathwork, and nervous system regulation into approachable practices that meet you exactly where you are. Whether you lead, serve, or simply seek peace in a busy world — you don’t need to be flexible, and you don’t need experience. You only need a willingness to return to yourself.

The body keeps the score — but it also holds the key to healing.

Our Philosophy

Yoga is for every body.

Strength includes softness.

Rest is productive.

Healing isn’t linear.

Movement can become meditation.

True wellness isn’t measured by how busy you are — but by how connected you remain to yourself through every season of life.

OUR CORE VALUES

The a.l.i.g.n.

Five principles that guide every practice, workshop and space we create.

A

Always Follow Your Authentic Path

Move with intention instead of expectation. Authenticity creates lasting alignment.

L

Lead with Love

Compassion, patience, humility and presence — spaces where every person feels seen.

I

Integrate Mind, Body & Breath

Transformation happens when movement, breath and awareness work as one.

G

God First

Faith is our foundation — gratitude, integrity and purpose in all we do.

N

Never Stop Growing

Alignment is a lifelong practice. We embrace curiosity, resilience and courage.
