Begin the Practice

Three phases. Five minutes. Right now.

Follow it straight through. The only rule: do not rush the exhales — that’s where the reset actually happens.

Phase 1 Breathwork

The Extended Exhale

2–4 minutes, the nervous system’s off switch

Inhale through the nose for 4. Exhale slowly through the mouth for 6. The longer exhale tells your body the emergency is over. Four to six rounds is enough.

Breath pacer, 4 in : 6 out
Expand with the circle on the inhale; soften on the longer exhale.

Phase 2 Movement & Release

Three Moves. Real Relief.

2–5 minutes, where men store stress most
01
Neck & Shoulder Release
Drop one ear toward the shoulder, breathe into the stretch 30–45s, switch. Release the screen-and-carry tension.
02
Hip Flexor Opener
Step into a gentle lunge, ease the hips forward, let each exhale open the front of the hip. 60–90s per side. This is the psoas — your stress muscle.
03
Seated Spinal Decompression
Fold forward over your thighs, let the head and arms hang, breathe into the low back. Undo hours of sitting.
Phase 3 Stillness

Simply Be Here.

1–6 minutes, the hardest part for most men

Not a nap. Not spacing out. A deliberate minute of presence. Run a quick body scan, then sit with the closing prompt.

Feet & legs — feel the floor, let them go.
Belly — stop bracing; release on the exhale.
Shoulders — drop them. Drop them again.
Jaw & face — unclench; soften the eyes.
Closing Prompt

“What is one thing I’ve been carrying today that I have the option to set down right now?”
